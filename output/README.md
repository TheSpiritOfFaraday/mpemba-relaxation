The PDFs and images are prepared research outputs. Their presence does not mean
every computation was rerun for publication. See [PACKAGE_CHECKS.json](../PACKAGE_CHECKS.json)
for the fresh scientific checks and [PRIVACY_REPORT.json](../PRIVACY_REPORT.json)
for the publication privacy audit.

Document metadata was removed for publication; mathematical PDF page content and
image pixels were preserved. [ANONYMIZATION.json](../ANONYMIZATION.json) records
the transformations. To regenerate outputs in a disposable copy, follow
[REPRODUCING.md](../REPRODUCING.md).
