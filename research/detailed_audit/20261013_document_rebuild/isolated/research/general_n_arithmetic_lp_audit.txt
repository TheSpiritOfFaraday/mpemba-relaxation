INDEPENDENT AUDIT: FIXED-MODE GRAPH-COUPLING LP

The reduction in general_n_arithmetic_lp.tex is mathematically valid on its
intended higher-dimensional domain. One statement-level correction was sent to
the author: explicitly require n>=4 (n>=3 suffices for the algebra). For n=2 the
normalization sum ell=1 is impossible and the first-three inequality is undefined.
Do not leave the intended dimensional restriction implicit in surrounding prose.

The reconstructed backward operator is L0=H B eta^{-1} H^T D, hence L0 H=H B
and L0*1=0. Since eta B is symmetric, B eta^{-1} is symmetric; consequently
D L0 is symmetric. Thus M0=L0^T is the correct column generator convention,
conserves probability, and satisfies detailed balance with q, although its
physical off-diagonal rates may have either sign.

The displayed energy identity is exact: each graph edge contributes
ell_ij[(x_j/x_i)y_i^2+(x_i/x_j)y_j^2-2y_i y_j]. With ell>=0 and x>0 this
is nonnegative, and its kernel consists precisely of vectors y whose ratios
y_i/x_i are constant on connected components. Connectivity is exactly the
simple-zero-eigenvalue condition. The diagonal similarity X^{-1}BX is a
reversible backward Markov generator, with weights eta_i*x_i^2 as stated.

Adding rho(q*1^T-I) shifts every contrast eigenvalue by -rho, leaves F/G
unchanged, and makes all physical rates positive for a sufficiently large
finite rho because every q_i>0. Hence the connected graph realizes Hx exactly
as the unique slow left mode. Conversely strict original G makes every
contrast off-diagonal positive by the established Helmert identity. Shifting
the slow eigenvalue to zero reconstructs exactly the displayed ell form; time
rescaling supplies sum ell=1. A finite minimum of strictly positive margins
then gives a positive LP margin, and vice versa.

Strictification is valid: the detailed-balanced generator with every downward
rate one has F=(t-1)(1+u/2)<0 and G=t-1<0 for all distinct ordered bath weights.
A sufficiently small addition preserves an existing strict arithmetic violation
and simple spectral ordering. It also makes every contrast off-diagonal
positive, so Perron coordinates become strictly positive. This is a global
existence argument, not a claim that a fixed boundary mode itself admits the
strict LP. The final cut-constraint paragraph correctly distinguishes the weak
fixed-mode problem and its connectivity requirement.

Rational density is justified because the construction parameterizes the mode
exactly rather than perturbing an arbitrary matrix and hoping its eigenvector
stays rational. Normalize q and ell in their affine simplexes, choose nearby
rational q,x,ell within the open set of strict inequalities, and reconstruct
B and M0 from them. Every eta_i and x_i denominator stays positive. A rational
sufficiently large reset may also be selected, yielding a fully rational
physical generator and an exactly rational Hx. All tests are rational functions,
so clearing positive denominators gives an existential polynomial system at
fixed n. This proves a decision reduction for arithmetic-inequality violation;
it does not prove that inequality, compute the thermal amplitude supremum, or
make a violating mode automatically violate a proposed thermal bound.
