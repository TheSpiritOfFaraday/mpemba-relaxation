BOUNDED HIGH-DIMENSION AMPLITUDE NOVELTY AUDIT -- 2026-10-11

RESULT
No matching 1-3/n achievable amplitude advantage, or near-unit ordinary-cooling
advantage under failure of EVERY original triplet test, was found in this
bounded primary-source search. This supports cautious comparison language,
not an absolute priority claim. Generic amplitude suppression, engineered
strong Mpemba effects, and Markov state aggregation are established ideas.

EXACT CLAIM COMPARED
For each fixed n>=4 the project constructs distinct energies and positive
reversible rates at beta_b=1, all original F_ijk,G_ijk strictly negative, a
simple slow rate with a gap bounded below, and finite cooling preparations
with 1-|b_h|/|b_c| approaching 1-3/n. The leading KL advantage tends to 1-9/n^2.
This is an achievable limit, not a proved sharp upper bound when n>4. The
no-dimension-independent-bound consequence takes the fixed-n limit first.
It is not a statement that relaxation times improve by the same percentage.

3. Klich, Raz, Hirschberg, Vucelja, The Mpemba index and anomalous relaxation,

The earlier full-text review establishes the strong-effect framework, including

test fails, with positive rates, distinct energies and a controlled slow gap.

4. Carollo, Lasanta, Lesanovsky, Exponentially accelerated approach to stationarity

arXiv:2103.05020v1, 2021-03-08; Phys. Rev. Lett. 127, 060401 (2021).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.05020
Live primary abstract and version history checked. It engineers initial unitary
rotations to eliminate overlap with the slowest mode. This independently shows
why a broad novelty claim about amplitude engineering would be inappropriate.
Those quantum preparations differ from the classical Gibbs-only preparation
curve and do not impose the original triplet tests. Only the abstract and
metadata were checked in this focused pass, not its entire proof.

5. Rios-Monje, Patron-Castro, Plata, Sanchez-Rey, Prados,

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12955
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12955v1
Primary abstract checked; HTML was accessible but its full proof was not audited.
The optimization concerns preparation of

chain, nor a result under the 2026 F/G inequalities. The checked live history
lists v1 only. This is a related optimization paper, not a resolution of the
present construction's priority.
