Independent all-n extension of the repeated-slow boundary classification
Date: 2026-10-11. Verifier agent. No report/source-generator file edited.

RESULT
The proposed generalization is TRUE for every n>=4 and FALSE in dimension3.
For n>=4, let M be an irreducible reversible generator with distinct ordered
energies, positive finite bath, all original linear tests F_ijk,G_ijk<=0,
and spectrum
  0>lambda2=...=lambda_(n-1)>lambda_n.
Then M is exactly a reset generator plus additional reversible mixing on one
adjacent pair. Consequently its thermal ordering is ordinary at all finite
times for every convex f-divergence, with strictness for strictly convex f
and distinct preparations on the same side of the bath. The adjacent pair
is unique because the fast eigenspace is one-dimensional.

F,G are stated as the primary hypotheses to make sense even if initially
some rates vanish. For strictly positive rates they are equivalent to failure
of the original branch predicates. An assertion of that equivalence directly
at zero rates needs care because the original kappa denominator may vanish.
The theorem's conclusion itself implies strictly positive rates.

1. RANK-ONE FORM
Normalize the bath weights q1>...>qn>0 to sum to one. Set D=diag(q),
rho=-lambda2>0, delta=lambda2-lambda_n>0. Spectral resolution gives
  M=rho(q1^T-I)-delta Dzz^T/N,
  q^Tz=0, N=z^TDz>0,
where z is the fast left eigenfunction. The original-rate formulas in any
dimension are
  G_ijk=-(delta/N) q_j z_k(z_i-z_j),
  F_ijk=(delta/N)[(q_i-q_j)z_i z_j
     -z_k(q_i z_i-q_j z_j+(q_k/2)(z_i-z_j))].
These do not involve any omitted-state effective rates.

2. HIGHEST-SUPPORT PROOF: ALL n>=4
Let m be the highest index with z_m nonzero and orient z_m>0. Zero bath
mean excludes m=1. All entries after m vanish.

Suppose m>=4. For every i<j<m, G_ijm<=0 implies z_i>=z_j, so
  z1>=z2>=...>=z_(m-1).
If the first m-2 entries were not all equal, some i<j<=m-2 would have
z_i>z_j. The distinct triplet (i,j,m-1) would then force z_(m-1)>=0.
Every preceding entry would be nonnegative, z_m would be positive, and
all later entries zero. This contradicts q^Tz=0 with q_i>0.
Hence z1=...=z_(m-2)=a.

There are at least two such equal entries because m>=4. The triplet (1,2,m)
gives a(a-z_m)<=0, so 0<=a<=z_m. Zero mean now forces z_(m-1)<0.
The different triplet (1,2,m-1) gives a(a-z_(m-1))<=0. Its second factor
is strictly positive and a>=0, so a=0. Thus the entire fast support is
exactly the adjacent pair {m-1,m}.

Suppose m=3. A trailing state4 exists because n>=4. G123 gives z1>=z2,
and centering with z3>0 forces z2<0. Since z4=0, F124<=0 implies
z1 z2<=0 and hence z1>=0. F134<=0 implies z1 z3<=0 and hence z1<=0.
Therefore z1=0 and support is exactly {2,3}.

Suppose m=2. Centering immediately gives z1<0 and support {1,2}.
These cases exhaust every possible highest nonzero index. There is no
induction assumption and no division by a component that might vanish.
This directly proves the result for n=5 as well as all larger n.

3. CONVERSE AND CANONICAL FORM
For support {k,k+1}, normalize z_k=-1/q_k,z_(k+1)=1/q_(k+1).
Then M=rho R+delta J^(k), where the only off-diagonal entries of J are
  J_(k,k+1)=q_k/(q_k+q_(k+1)),
  J_(k+1,k)=q_(k+1)/(q_k+q_(k+1)),
and the diagonal conserves probability. Its extra pair relaxation rate is
delta. The full spectrum is 0, -rho repeated n-2 times, and -rho-delta.
All off-diagonal entries of the full M are strictly positive because rho>0.

Put d=delta/(q_k+q_(k+1)). Across all n choose3 triplets the only nonzero
original linear tests are
  F_(k,k+1,l)=-(q_k-q_(k+1))d for l>k+1,
  F_(i,k,k+1)=-(q_k+q_(k+1)/2)d for i<k,
  G_(i,k,k+1)=-q_k d for i<k.
They are all strictly negative; every other F/G is zero. This proves the
converse directly, including boundary pairs. The classification is therefore
an if-and-only-if statement.

The adjacent-difference matrix for the backward generator is -rho I plus
one nonzero column k: diagonal -delta, upper neighbor delta*q_(k+1)/Qpair,
and lower neighbor delta*q_k/Qpair, omitting nonexistent boundary neighbors.
It is Metzler for every n. Thus stochastic monotonicity and the established
reversible convex-divergence comparison apply in all dimensions. No simple
slow eigenvector is selected within the repeated space.

4. EXACT FIVE-STATE CHECK EXECUTED
As an independent check of the general converse indexing, used
  q=(16,8,4,2,1)/31, rho=2, delta=3, adjacent pair {3,4}.
Constructed M from the rank-one formula with exact SymPy rationals.
Checked conservation, detailed balance, positivity of all20 off-diagonal
rates, all ten original F/G triplets, and characteristic polynomial
  lambda*(lambda+2)^3*(lambda+5).
All passed. This example is a verification aid; the all-n necessity is the
support proof above, not an extrapolation from this computation.

5. WHY n>=4 IS NECESSARY: EXACT THREE-STATE COUNTEREXAMPLE
Let beta_b=1, energies (0,log2,2log2), q=(4,2,1)/7, and downward rates
  a12=1, a13=2, a23=3.
Then the positive reversible column generator is
  M=[[-1,  1,   2],
     [1/2,-5/2, 3],
     [1/2, 3/2,-5]].
The sole triplet has t=u=1/2,
  kappa=10/3, F=-2, G=-2,
so its original predicate fails strictly. Exact characteristic polynomial:
  lambda*(2lambda^2+17lambda+28)/2.
The two nonzero eigenvalues are (-17+sqrt65)/4 and (-17-sqrt65)/4,
distinct and negative. Thus the slow multiplicity is n-2=1 and there is
exactly one fast direction, as the proposed all-n hypothesis requires.

It is not reset plus adjacent mixing. If the extra pair were {1,2}, the
unmodified downward rates a13,a23 would satisfy a13/a23=q1/q2=2, whereas
their actual ratio is2/3. If the pair were {2,3}, reset alone would supply
both a12 and a13, requiring a12=a13, whereas they are1 and2. Both possible
adjacent pairs are excluded. Exact checks of reversibility and characteristic
polynomial were executed. The missing fourth-state F124/F134 constraints
are precisely why the m=3 support argument cannot work in dimension3.

No substantive obstruction remains for n>=4. Publication novelty and more
general spectral multiplicities with two or more fast directions were not
investigated by this audit.
