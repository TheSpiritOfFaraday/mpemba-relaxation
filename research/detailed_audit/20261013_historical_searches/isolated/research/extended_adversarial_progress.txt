EXTENDED ADVERSARIAL RESULT: SHARP QUARTER BOUND VIA AN INDEPENDENT STRUCTURAL LEMMA

Status: mathematical proof below; exact symbolic identities checked by
research/extended_arithmetic_certificate.py. This is not a claim resting on the
preceding numerical search. The parent has an independent Schur-complement proof.

1. Statement and scope

Let q1>q2>q3>q4>0 be (possibly unnormalized) bath weights. Let M be a reversible
four-state generator, with a simple slow nonstationary eigenvalue and all original
triplet predicates failing. The established branch-equivalence lemma means all
F_ijk<=0 and G_ijk<=0. Orient its slow left eigenvector w by the nonnegative
weighted-Helmert coordinates of the established G/Metzler theorem. Then

    w1 >= w2,    w1+w2 >= 2*w3,    w4 <= 0.

Only the middle inequality is new here. It implies that the conditional Gibbs
expectation on the first three states increases with beta>=0. Together with the
parent's decomposition, it proves that every finite cooling amplitude advantage
is strictly below 25%. The quarter-limit construction therefore proves sharpness
as a supremum for the full original predicate premise.

2. Reduction to strictly positive Helmert coordinates

It suffices first to prove the middle inequality when all G inequalities are
strict. Under this hypothesis all off-diagonal entries of the symmetric
weighted-Helmert matrix A are positive, by the established formula in
ordered_conductance_theorem.tex. Perron theory then gives strictly positive slow
coordinates. Normalize the coefficient of the first unnormalized contrast to 1,
and write

    w = h1 + b*h2 + c*h3,    b,c>0.

For arbitrary weak F/G, add delta times the reversible generator H whose six
original downward rates all equal 1. Each triplet has

    F(H)=-(1-t)*(1+u/2)<0,    G(H)=t-1<0.

The perturbed generator retains positivity and reversibility, all its F/G are
strict, and its slow eigenvalue stays simple for sufficiently small delta>0.
Orient and normalize slow eigenvectors continuously using positive weighted norm
and positive overlap with the original oriented eigenvector. Passing delta->0
preserves the non-strict arithmetic inequality. This handles zero Helmert
coordinates without division by them. It does not claim a unique mode for a
degenerate slow eigenspace.

3. Three nonnegative coupling parameters

Write q=(p,q,r,h), where p>q>r>h>0, and Q2=p+q, Q3=p+q+r,
Q4=p+q+r+h. These q-symbols are bath weights, not transition rates.

The unnormalized Helmert contrast matrix is

    H = [[1,       1,           1],
         [-p/q,    1,           1],
         [0,      -Q2/r,        1],
         [0,       0,          -Q3/h]].

Its weighted Gram matrix is N=diag(eta1,eta2,eta3), with
eta1=p*Q2/q, eta2=Q2*Q3/r, eta3=Q3*Q4/h.

Subtract the slow eigenvalue from the nonstationary generator by a reset shift.
This preserves every F and G, leaves off-diagonal Helmert entries unchanged, and
makes v=(1,b,c) a zero eigenvector of its Helmert coefficient matrix K. Weighted
self-adjointness and strict Metzler positivity give

    K_ij = ell_ij/eta_i,   ell_ij=ell_ji>0,
    K_ii = -sum_{j!=i} ell_ij*v_j/(eta_i*v_i).

Conversely these formulas reconstruct the shifted function generator as
L=H*K*N^{-1}*H^T*diag(q), with M=L^T. No sign assumption on the shifted physical
rates is needed; all triplet expressions are still the original linear F/G.

Direct substitution gives

    G123 = q/(p*Q3)*[-ell12+(h/Q4)*ell13],
    G124 = -q/(p*Q4)*ell13,
    G134 = -r/(Q2*Q4)*[(q/p)*ell13+ell23],
    G234 = r/(Q2*Q4)*[ell13-ell23].

Consequently write

    ell12 = u+(h/Q4)*m,  ell13=m,  ell23=v0+m,
    u>=0, v0>=0, m>0.

The symbol v0 here is a scalar coupling remainder, not the mode vector v.

4. Contradiction if the arithmetic contrast were negative

The desired arithmetic inequality is

    w1+w2-2*w3 = -(p-q)/q + 2*b*Q3/r >=0.

Suppose instead 0<b<s, where s=(p-q)*r/(2*q*Q3).
The exact original F123 expression becomes

    F123 = A*u+B*m+C*v0,

where

 A = -[b*(p-q)+2*p]*[2*b*q*Q3-(p-q)*r]/[2*b*p*Q2^2*Q3],
 B = -(p-q)*[b*q*Q3-p*r]*[b*h+c*Q4+h]/[b*p*Q2^2*Q3*Q4],
 C = (p-q)*[c*r*Q4-b*h*Q2]/[b*Q2^2*Q3*Q4].

Because b<s, A>0 and B>0. Since m>0 and F123<=0, necessarily v0>0 and C<0.
Thus

    0<c<cmax=b*h*Q2/(r*Q4).

In particular b*Q2-c*r>0. Set k=(b*Q2-c*r)/(c*Q3)>0. The coefficient of v0
in F124 equals -k*C, and its coefficient of u is

 D = -(p-q)*[b*Q3+r]*[b*q*Q3-p*r]/[b*p*Q2^2*Q3^2]>0.

Therefore the nonpositive combination F124+k*F123 has the form

    F124+k*F123 = (D+k*A)*u + [N(c)/(2*c*p*Q2*Q3*Q4)]*m.

It suffices to prove N(c)>0 on the indicated domain. The identity checker forms
N directly from the original generator and proves the following exact formulas:

    coefficient of c^2 in N(c) = -2*q*(p-q)*Q4 <0,
    N(0)=2*h*(b+1)*(p-q)*(p-b*q)>0.

The remaining endpoint N(cmax), as a function of b, is also a concave quadratic:

    coefficient of b^2 =
      -2*h*q*(h+r)*(p-q)*Q3^2/[r^2*Q4] <0,
    N(cmax)|_{b=0} = 2*h*p*(p-q)>0,
    N(cmax)|_{b=s} = h*r*(p-q)*Q2^2/[2*q*Q4]>0.

A concave function on a compact interval is at least the smaller endpoint
value. Hence N(cmax)>0 for 0<=b<=s, then N(c)>0 for 0<=c<=cmax. The displayed
combination is strictly positive because m>0, contradicting F124<=0 and F123<=0.
This proves the arithmetic lemma in the strict-G case, and Section 2 extends it
to weak inequalities.

5. From the arithmetic lemma to the sharp amplitude bound

Let w1=A, w2=B, w3=C. Then A>=B and C<=(A+B)/2. For any ordered three energies,
the conditional covariance Cov_beta(w,E) is nonpositive for beta>=0. One direct
proof sets energies to (0,a,b), 0<a<b. If B>=C every pair difference has the
required sign. Otherwise B<C<=A, and after multiplying the pairwise covariance
by the positive factor Z3^2*exp(beta*b), its negative is

  a*(A-B)*exp(beta*(b-a))
  + b*(A-C)
  - (b-a)*(C-B)*exp(-beta*a).

This expression is increasing with beta, and at beta=0 equals

  a*(A-B) + b*(A-C) - (b-a)*(C-B)
  = (3*a/2)*(A-B)+(b-a/2)*(A+B-2*C) >=0.

[The identity is exact.] Thus the conditional mean m(beta) is nondecreasing.

Let P(beta) be the Gibbs probability of the first three states; it is increasing,
with P(beta)>3/4 for beta>0. The full overlap is
b(beta)=P(beta)*m(beta)+(1-P(beta))*w4. The G theorem gives b(beta)<0 on cooling
and w4<=0. For 0<beta_h<beta_c<1 and r0=P(beta_c)/P(beta_h),

 b_c-r0*b_h = P_c*(m_c-m_h)+(1-r0)*w4 >=0.

Consequently |b_c|/|b_h|<=r0<4/3 and 1-|b_h|/|b_c|<1/4 whenever there is an
anomalous amplitude ordering. The existing quarter-limit family attains this
bound as a supremum.

7. Rejected route

The mixed-coordinate cone using w1-w2, w1+w2-2w3, and the weighted prefix-three
contrast is not preserved by a general allowed generator: a transformed
lower-left entry contains a negative multiple of a34. A direct Metzler
invariance argument in those three coordinates therefore does not work.
The successful proof instead prescribes the Perron mode, uses three nonnegative
couplings, and eliminates one coupling using F123 and F124.

8. Further exact result: the new mode inequalities are NOT sufficient

Take unnormalized q=(5,4,2,1) and w=(162,-18,71,-880). Then q dot w=0,
w1>w2, w1+w2-2*w3=2>0 and w4<0. Its unnormalized Helmert coefficients
are (80,2,80), all strictly positive. Nevertheless this direction cannot be a
simple slow mode under the full original triplet premise.

An independent rational Farkas certificate is in
research/extended_mode_obstruction_certificate.py and its JSON/log. Let

 U = [[-89/450,   431/450],
      [-91/360,  -521/360],
      [1,          0],
      [0,          1]],
 M0 = -diag(q)*U*B*U^T.

The inverse-realizability theorem says a simple slow realization requires
B positive definite. Exact substitution proves

 (482571629/11676414)*F123(M0)
 +(496382581/11676414)*F124(M0)
 +(16620682809/77842760)*G234(M0) = trace(B).

All three coefficients are strictly positive. The triplet inequalities would
therefore imply trace(B)<=0, contradicting positive definiteness. The certificate
checks the identity, null vectors, bath mean, all strict necessary-mode
inequalities, and the positive Helmert coefficient decomposition using rational
arithmetic only. Thus the arithmetic structural lemma sharpens the feasible
mode region but does not replace the exact disk/half-plane characterization.
