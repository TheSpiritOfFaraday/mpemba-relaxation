The claim is correct. Independent exact calculations are in
research/reset_trace_independent_audit.py and its JSON receipt. This script
reconstructs the full generator directly from the physical reduced rates in the
original receipt; it does not reuse the original sequential elimination routine.

1. The bath weights are (100,4,3,2,1)/110, strictly positive and ordered.
All physical off-diagonal rates are strictly positive. Conservation and detailed
balance hold exactly. The minimum physical reduced conductance is exactly1.
The largest (least negative) original F is -24033/27500000; the largest original

used: the triplet comparisons here are the linear F/G expressions.

T=N_AA-N_AB*(N_BB)^(-1)*N_BA.
The eliminated block determinant is
2198102257945001/60500000000000000>0.
The resulting T is conservative and reversible for the retained bath weights.
Its effective-rate F123 equals

whereas its effective G123 remains negative. The original-rate tests and these
trace-rate tests are different quantities. In particular this is not a direct

Replacing the actual slow spectral parameter by zero loses a potentially
essential correction. This does not invalidate a proof using the actual
spectral parameter or another comparison argument.

CONDITIONAL HIGH-DIMENSION BOUND FRAGMENT

I reread high_dimension_bound_reduction.tex against the actual theorem in
ordered_conductance_theorem.tex. The normalized Helmert basis in that theorem
and the unnormalized contrasts used by the reduction differ only by positive
factors sqrt(eta_k), so nonnegative slow coordinates carry over. Every contrast
has zero bath mean and strictly negative cooling overlap. The grouped first two
contrasts are supported on123 and have zero conditional bath mean; higher
contrasts are constant on123, so they do not affect either arithmetic difference.
The displayed conditional-mean derivatives are strictly positive for nonzero
first-three group satisfying the added arithmetic inequality. The highest
full-support contrast is handled separately with P_n=1. The final overlap
ratio is a positive weighted average, so the strict n/3 bound is valid.

The supremum bracket and scope are correct: 1-3/n <= S_n <= 1-2/n under full
F/G and a simple slow mode. The first-three inequality is an additional
hypothesis for the conditional proposition, not a consequence asserted for all
n. The four-state equality S_4=1/4 uses the separate established four-state
arithmetic theorem. No correction to the fragment is needed.
