1. General G cone, near-bath KL
240 independent generators, 40 each in dimensions 3,4,5,6,7,8. Bath beta_b=1.
Ordered energies obtained from positive loguniform gaps (10^-2 to 10), then
rescaled to a uniformly sampled total span between 1 and 15. Bath weights are
normalized exp(-E). For each upper column j, independently sampled reduced
conductances in [10^-3,10^3] are sorted increasing with i<j; symmetry completes
k_ij. This enforces every G_ijk<=0. Generator offdiagonals are M_ij=q_i k_ij.
All sampled offdiagonals positive, min 3.2586e-10. Largest raw column-sum residual
1.2e-13; detailed balance and contrast Metzler structure checked numerically.

For z=|beta-1|*(E_n-E_1) in {.0001,.05,.25,.5,.8,2/sqrt(5)}, both heating and
cooling were tested. All beta remain positive because span>=1. For each use
tau=t*|lambda_2| in {0,.001,.01,.1,1,3,10}. Total 20,160 KL outward-derivative
checks: all positive, zero rejected for tiny signal, zero violations.
Minimum derivative / sum(abs(component contributions)) = 0.9948480451.

2. Physical tridiagonal contrast generators, heating convex divergences
Another 240 independent generators, same dimension and energy ranges. Start
with a real symmetric tridiagonal orthonormal Helmert contrast matrix whose
positive offdiagonals and negative diagonal magnitudes lie in [10^-2,10^2].
Lift it to a reversible zero-column-sum matrix, then add rho*(q*1^T-I), choosing
rho one unit above the largest reduced-rate deficit. This makes all physical
offdiagonals strictly positive and shifts only the contrast diagonal. All 240
also pass the original G tests numerically (tolerance 10^-10).
Min physical offdiagonal 3.2242e-7; largest raw column-sum residual 1.6e-11.

Heating z in {.001,.05,.3,1,3,8}, same seven normalized times. 10,080 evaluations
per divergence. KL, chi-square, reverse KL, squared Hellinger, quartic (r-1)^4,
and total variation |r-1| were tested by sum q_i (dr_i/d beta) Phi'(r_i).
TV uses its derivative away from kinks, not a dedicated test of kink points.
All accepted derivatives positive; no negative values. Exactly 320 quartic
values had absolute contribution sum below 10^-24 and were REJECTED as tiny,
not accepted as zeros. All other 60,160 tridiagonal derivative checks accepted.
Smallest normalized signed values by divergence:
  KL .3845313; chi-square .2322931; reverse KL .7315716;
  Hellinger .6235952; quartic .4933675; total variation .01231246.
Negative-violation threshold: -10^-10 times sum(abs(component contributions)).
The explicit signal cutoff prevents underflow-sized values counting as success.

3. Independent numerical formulation
Main evolution uses an orthogonal spectral decomposition in the weighted
Helmert contrast space; probability derivatives are analytically differentiated
Gibbs initial states with bath and generator held fixed. At 12 points in each
family, independently compute exp(t M), propagate the probability and its
derivative, and compare with spectral results. Also use centered finite
differences in beta with h=10^-5/max(1,energy span).
Largest relative KL-derivative discrepancy, expm vs spectral:
  general G: 1.67e-13; tridiagonal: 2.23e-15.
Largest relative finite-difference discrepancy:
  general G: 4.30e-7; tridiagonal: 4.11e-6.
Every spot check passed the explicit 10^-7 / 10^-5 tolerances respectively.

4. Ratio-order obstruction for a broader TP2-style proof
At points where all contrast x,y coordinates exceed 10^-12, test whether the
heating derivative ratios y_k/x_k decrease in k (relative tolerance 10^-8).
There were zero observed failures for the tridiagonal family, but 2,235 failures
for the general-G family. The first n=5 example is fully specified in the audit
JSON, including q, E, physical M, beta and physical time. Rebuilding a reversible
generator from those decimal upper rates and checking at 80 decimal digits gives
final consecutive ratios 124674.89514246866 < 124674.99188580388, increase
0.0967433352201991. KL outward derivative is still +6.23659510031135e-8.
Thus ratio ordering does not numerically extend to arbitrary general-G dynamics;
this is not a KL counterexample. The high precision result is not an interval
certificate and should be described as a verified numerical obstruction only.
