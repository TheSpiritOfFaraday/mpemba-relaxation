TARGETED NEXT CALCULATION
The viable direct route is a fast-space PSD alternative: for an arbitrary
prescribed Perron-cone mode with w1+w2-2w3<0, seek a nonnegative combination of
original F/G matrices that is nonzero positive semidefinite on the(q,w)-orthogonal
fast space. Such a dual certificate excludes every positive definite fast
matrix and directly proves the missing arithmetic implication. Merely testing
Schur trace at spectral parameter zero is insufficient, as shown above.

accelerated adjacent pair, where several slow rates coincide. Project a
premise-preserving perturbation onto that repeated eigenspace and classify its
largest eigenvector. This removes the irrelevant original fast direction and
can expose a limiting arithmetic violation without ill-conditioned subtraction.

PROVED RESTRICTED COMPRESSION LEMMA
Suppose the full n-state system satisfies the premise and has a simple slow
left mode w whose components on states4,...,n all equal one constant c.
Put Q=sum_{k=4}^n q_k. If Q<q3, then w1+w2>=2w3 follows from the established
four-state theorem, without any assumption that the full chain is lumpable.

Indeed compress functions onto those constant on the tail block, using the
q-weighted orthogonal projection. The resulting four-state generator keeps
all low-low rates and has
  a_(i,T) = sum_k(q_k/Q) a_(i,k),
  a_(T,i) = sum_k a_(k,i) = (Q/q_i) a_(i,T).
It is positive and reversible with bath weights(q1,q2,q3,Q). The full slow
mode belongs to this compression subspace, so it remains an eigenvector.
Rayleigh-Ritz shows no compressed nonstationary eigenvalue exceeds the full
lambda2, and simplicity of the full eigenvalue implies simplicity after
compression. Hence it is the compressed slow mode.
For i<j<=3, direct substitution into the ORIGINAL predicates gives
  G_(ijT) = sum_k (q_k/Q) G_(ijk),
  F_(ijT) = sum_k (q_k/Q) F_(ijk)
             +sum_k q_k(Q-q_k)/(2Q q_j) G_(ijk).
All added coefficients are nonnegative, so every compressed triplet fails;
triplet123 is unchanged. The condition Q<q3 ensures the four compressed bath
weights remain strictly energy-ordered. The proved four-state arithmetic
lemma now applies. This restriction covers a well-separated rare tail block
with constant slow components, including the unperturbed cloning construction.
It does not settle unequal tail components, and aggregation can reorder the
energies when Q>=q3. That is a concrete remaining case for a compression proof.
