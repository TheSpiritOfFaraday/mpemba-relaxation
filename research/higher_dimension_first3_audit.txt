SKEPTICAL AUDIT: FIRST-THREE ARITHMETIC RESTRICTION IN HIGHER DIMENSION

Status: numerical adversarial search only; no proof and no counterexample.
The four-state proof must not be promoted to all n on this evidence.

We tested whether the full original F/G premise can coexist with a simple
Perron slow eigenfunction satisfying w1+w2<2w3. In weighted Helmert coordinates
w=Hx, this is x2<s*x1, s=(q1-q2)q3/(2q2(q1+q2+q3)).

For each fixed ordered positive q and strictly positive offending x, construct
K_ij=ell_ij/eta_i (i!=j),
K_ii=-sum_{j!=i}ell_ij*x_j/(eta_i*x_i), with ell_ij=ell_ji>=0.
Then Kx=0, K is diagonally symmetrizable Metzler, and with connected positive
edges its zero Perron eigenvalue is simple. Reconstruct the shifted physical
matrix M=(H K eta^{-1} H^T diag(q))^T. Every original F/G is linear in ell.
A sufficiently large positive reset addition makes every physical rate positive,
while preserving all F/G, the eigenfunction, and its simple slow ordering.
Thus a strictly feasible LP would be a genuine route to a counterexample.

The LP maximized a common scaled strict margin for all F/G and every graph edge,
with sum ell=1. The executed search used seed723401, 20,000 n=5 attempts and
10,000 n=6 attempts, varied ordered bath gaps, x2/s in (.05,.999), and independent
positive tail coordinates across multiple logarithmic scales. No strictly
feasible candidate survived independent checks of original LP residuals and
variable bounds. The retained script fully specifies distributions and settings:
  research/higher_dimension_first3_search.py
  research/higher_dimension_first3_search.log

A preliminary apparent LP success was rejected: HiGHS default tolerance allowed
a negative graph coupling of about -3.855e-8, destroying the Metzler hypothesis.
That record is retained as higher_dimension_first3_rejected_lp_tolerance.json.
The final search tightened primal/dual tolerances to1e-10 and required both
all edge values and all F/G residual margins to exceed90% of the claimed margin.
No mathematical conclusion rests on solver infeasibility or tiny residuals.

Potential proof avenue, not established: in this shifted graph representation,
edges entirely among tail Helmert coordinates act proportionally to a reset on
states1,2,3 and cancel from F123. A positive linear combination of F123 and
F12k may generalize the four-state graph dual proof, but the signs of remaining
tail-tail contributions need a new argument. The existing scalar Schur formula
cannot simply replace the tail inverse by a scalar when n>4.
